## Considered and not used
- (superseded, AAN now used, see table) **Harvard AAN atlas v2.0** (Dryad doi:10.5061/dryad.zw3r228d2, CC0, has PAG, LC, DR, PBC, LDTg, mRt...): the Dryad file API returns 401 without a bearer token and I did not create an account. Owner decision: download the NIfTIs by hand (free Dryad login) and drop them in `_atlas/raw/aan/`; they are in MNI152 1 mm space and would replace the derived PAG and add LC/raphe/etc.
- **Brainstem Navigator v1.0** (Bianciardi lab, NITRC; downloaded by the owner 2026-11-07, unzipped to `_atlas/raw/brainstem-navigator/`): **NOT USED, licence forbids publishing it.** `Documentation/Copyright.txt`: (1) "use ... without charge for non-commercial research purposes only", verbatim copies only for personal/organisational use with the notices; (2) "YOU MAY NOT DISTRIBUTE COPIES of the Brainstem Navigator files, or copies of files or of information derived from them, to others outside your organization"; (3) research only, not clinical; (4) publications must cite the papers in the Readme (Bianciardi 2015 Brain Connect 5:597; Bianciardi 2018 NeuroImage 170:222; Garcia-Gomar 2019 Front Neurosci 13:764; Singh 2020 Front Neurosci 13:1425; Singh 2021 Brain Connect 11:613; Garcia-Gomar 2021 Mov Disord). Serving derived label volumes or meshes to students from a public site is distribution of derived information, so nothing from it is in `public/` or any committed file. Content also would not have covered the owner's list: it has Ve (vestibular complex), VSM (medullary viscero-sensory-motor complex = NTS/DMX/XII together), ION, SOC, LC, raphe, PAG, RN, SN, reticular parts, PBN, CnF, but no separate facial/abducens/trochlear/oculomotor/trigeminal/gracile/cuneate nuclei. Possible route: ask Marta Bianciardi (MGH Martinos) for written permission to redistribute derived meshes in an educational web app.
- **SUIT / Diedrichsen cerebellar nuclei** (DiedrichsenLab/cerebellar_atlases): licence file in the repo is CC BY-NC 3.0 ("SUIT template, associated files and atlases"), and the nuclei atlas lives in SUIT space (needs a non-linear SUIT to MNI warp). Not used: non-commercial terms on a public site, and no deformation field was pulled. The dentate nucleus therefore stays "cerebellar deep nuclei" (regional).
- **JHU ICBM-DTI-81 / XTRACT / CIT168**: distributed with FSL / need a licence click-through; the AHRA + 80-bundle pair already covers the same gaps. CIT168 would only refine SN into pars compacta/reticulata.
- **SUIT / Diedrichsen cerebellar atlases** (GitHub, no licence file): lobular parcellations, no nuclei; dentate is therefore only shown as "cerebellar deep nuclei".

## Derived structures (documented in `tools/atlas/build_atlas2.py`)
- Periaqueductal grey: AHRA midbrain-tegmentum voxels within 4 mm of the aqueduct.
- Internal capsule: AHRA forebrain white matter within 4.5 mm of both (caudate+thalamus+hypothalamus) and (putamen+globus pallidus), opened and size-filtered; split at the anterior thalamic pole (genu = +/-3 mm).
- Longitudinal fibres of the pons: corticospinal/corticopontine density inside the AHRA basilar pons.
- "Regional" structures (cranial-nerve nuclei, pontine nuclei, dentate...) light up the whole enclosing AHRA region and are flagged as such in the UI.

## MNI to Z-Anatomy registration (`tools/atlas/register_mni_zanat.py`)
Affine fitted on centroids of 20 central landmarks (thalamus, putamen, pallidum, hippocampus, amygdala, ventricles, habenula, hypothalamus, pons, medulla, per hemisphere): RMS 2.94 mm, max 4.2 mm, leave-one-out RMS 3.68 mm. Held-out check (not in the fit): midbrain centroid 1.5 mm. Cortex, fornix, caudate, callosum and cuneus fit badly (9-18 mm; Z-Anatomy's BodyParts3D brain differs in shape), so only central structures got a registered 3D mesh (19 meshes, 380 KB); everything else stays MRI-only in the slice tab.
