The Lab 1 cards that introduce directional terms and planes (anterior/posterior, rostral/caudal, dorsal/ventral,
medial/lateral, superficial/deep; sagittal, coronal, horizontal; the neuraxis bend between cerebrum and brainstem) use
the course's slide images and feel "dusty". Streamline them and make our own visuals: crisp, consistent, theme-aware
figures (or small 3D/animated scenes on the Explore model) that show each term as an arrow pair on a body and on the
brain, the change of dorsal/ventral and rostral/caudal at the midbrain-diencephalon bend, and each plane cutting the
brain. Keep the cards' answers and the course's wording; only the pictures and the introduction flow change.
